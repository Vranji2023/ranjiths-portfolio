LandCase AI — AI-Powered Land Dispute & Litigation Tracker

The Real Problem:

Land litigation in India is notorious for taking years and generating mountains of dense, multi-page legal court orders, survey deeds, and petitions. Non-lawyers and property owners have almost no transparent way to track the actual status of a disputed parcel without getting lost in legal jargon.

What I Built & Led:

I stepped up as the Prompt Lead & Architect on our 3-person team. I designed the multi-turn prompt pipeline that ingests messy court summaries and legal claims, forcing deterministic extraction into strict JSON schemas so our application never hallucinated court orders or land survey numbers.

We built and iterated the entire prototype live inside the Google Antigravity IDE, debugging application state, hooking up Flask routes, and pitching the working demo to hackathon evaluators to clinch a finalist spot in HACKASTORM 2.0.

Dr.Dine — Smart Dietary Recommender & Ingredient OCR

The Real Problem:

Packaged foods hide hidden sugars, trans-fats, and allergens under obscure chemical names. People managing specific conditions (like high blood pressure, diabetes, or dietary allergies) can't easily decipher ingredient panels at the grocery store.

How I Built It in Two Iterations:

Version 1 (Web MVP): Started by building a full-stack Flask web application using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript with a custom rule-based recommendation algorithm to match calorie targets and medical profiles.

Version 2 (The ML Upgrade): Manually typing ingredients felt clunky, so I evolved it into a Streamlit application with Tesseract OCR integration. Users simply snap a picture of a packaged food label; the OCR extracts the raw text, and a Logistic Regression model (trained in Jupyter Notebook) predicts dietary risk and suggests customized meal alternatives.
